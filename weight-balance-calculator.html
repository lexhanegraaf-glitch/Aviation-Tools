For planning and training only — not a substitute for the approved AFM/POH and the aircraft's current weighing record. This tool computes weight, centre of gravity and moment from the profile and loading you enter, and tests the result against the CG envelope you supply. Results are only as accurate as those inputs: the empty weight and empty CG must come from the specific aircraft's latest weight-and-balance record.

CG is checked at takeoff and, after fuel burn, at landing and zero fuel, since the CG moves as fuel is used. The optional safety margin draws a dashed safe band inside the certified limits: inside it is green, between it and the limit is amber (legal but reduced buffer), and outside the limit is red. By default taxi and trip burn are zero, so the Takeoff figure equals the full ramp load you entered — set them if you want the after-burn cases. Always verify against the official documents; the pilot in command is solely responsible for ensuring the aircraft is loaded within limits.
