For planning and training only — not a substitute for the approved AFM/POH. Power, true airspeed and fuel-flow figures are interpolated from Figure 5-7 (Cruise Performance) of the Cessna R172K POH, valid at 2550 lb with the recommended lean mixture and cowl flap closed. The chart spans 2,000–12,000 ft pressure altitude, ISA−20 to ISA+20, and 2200–2600 RPM; values outside these ranges are clamped, and manifold pressures above what is achievable at altitude are limited to the chart maximum. For best fuel economy at 70% power or less, the POH directs leaning a further 1 GPH below the chart (or to peak EGT if an EGT gauge is fitted) — actual flow may then be lower than shown.

Endurance and range are cruise-only estimates and do not include taxi, takeoff, climb, descent or manoeuvring fuel, all of which must be added separately (see the climb and fuel-flow charts). Real performance varies with mixture technique, engine condition, airframe rigging and instrument error. Always cross-check against current data, plan a sensible reserve, and remember the pilot in command is solely responsible for fuel planning and the safe conduct of the flight.
